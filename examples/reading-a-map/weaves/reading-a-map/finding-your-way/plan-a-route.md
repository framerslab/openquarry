# Plan a route

A walk has three legs: 1 km from the car park to the bridge, 600 m from the bridge to the hilltop and 1.4 km from the hilltop back to the car park.

1. You walk 4 km in an hour. How long does the whole walk take without a stop?
2. You add a stop of 15 minutes at the hilltop and want to be back at the car park by 4:00 pm. What is the latest time you can set off?
3. At the bridge the path splits in two. How do you check which path to take?

## Answers

1. The walk is 3 km. At 4 km an hour, 3 km takes three quarters of an hour, which is 45 minutes.
2. 45 minutes of walking and 15 minutes of rest make 1 hour. Set off by 3:00 pm.
3. Orient the map: turn it until the bridge and one more thing you can see line up with the map. Then take the path that leaves the bridge the way the map shows.

# Check your map reading

Answer each question, then compare with the answers below.

1. Where on a map do you find out what a symbol stands for?
2. On a 1:50,000 map, two villages are 8 cm apart. How far apart are they on the ground?
3. A path is 2 km long. How long is it on a 1:25,000 map?
4. You can see a tower on your left. On the map in your hands the tower is on your right. What do you do?

## Answers

1. In the key, also called the legend.
2. 1 cm is 500 m, so 8 cm is 4,000 m. The villages are 4 km apart.
3. 1 km is 4 cm, so 2 km is 8 cm.
4. Turn the map until the tower on the map is on the same side as the real tower. The map is then oriented.

## Marking

Give one point for each right answer. With three or four points you are ready to plan a walk with a map.

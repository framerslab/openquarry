# Measure a walk

You have a 1:25,000 map. A walk has three legs. With a ruler you measure them on the map:

| Leg | On the map |
|---|---|
| Car park to the bridge | 4 cm |
| Bridge to the hilltop | 2.4 cm |
| Hilltop back to the car park | 5.6 cm |

1. How long is each leg on the ground?
2. How long is the whole walk?

## Answers

1. 1 cm on the map is 250 m on the ground, so the legs are 1,000 m, 600 m and 1,400 m.
2. 1,000 m + 600 m + 1,400 m = 3,000 m. The walk is 3 km.

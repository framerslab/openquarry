# What a map shows

A map is a drawing of the ground as seen from straight above. It leaves out most of what is there and keeps what helps you find your way: paths, roads, water, buildings and the shape of the land.

## Symbols and the key

A map draws each kind of thing with a symbol. On many maps a blue line is a stream and a dashed line is a footpath. The key, also called the legend, lists every symbol on the map and says what it stands for. Maps differ, so read the key of the map in your hand before you trust a symbol.

## Example

You see a thin blue line crossing a dashed black line. The key says the blue line is a stream and the dashed line is a footpath. So the path crosses a stream there.

## Check yourself

Pick three symbols on any map near you and find each one in its key.
